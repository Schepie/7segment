Browser & OS Compatibility

  • Android / Windows / Mac: Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Web Bluetooth works out-of-the-box without extra apps.
  • iPhone & iPad (iOS): Apple Safari blocks Web Bluetooth. Install the free Bluefy or WebBLE browser app from the App Store.
  • Offline Ready (PWA): The app caches automatically. It opens and works at the court even without an active internet connection.

Seamless Ecosystem Sync

  • Master Rules Engine: The ESP32 scoreboard serves as the single source of truth. Phone, physical clicker, and smartwatch stay 100% in sync.
  • Instant Multi-Device Feedback: A point clicked on the physical Bluetooth remote updates the web app and watch in milliseconds.
  • Remote MAC Management: Paired Xiaomi clicker MAC is viewable and configurable under the Info tab.
