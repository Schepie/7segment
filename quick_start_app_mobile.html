📱 Mobile App Guide
🎾 App Features & Navigation
Web Bluetooth companion controller for the ESP32 Padel Scoreboard. Real-time scoring, live server indicator, RTC clock sync, and match rules customization.
⚡ 3-Step Instant Connect
1

Open Web App

Launch schepie.github.io/7segment in Chrome (Android/PC/Mac) or Bluefy/WebBLE (iPhone/iPad).

2

Tap Connect

Tap the glowing blue [⚡ Connect] button in the top navigation header.

3

Select Scoreboard

Choose ESP32_SCOREBOARD from the Bluetooth browser dialog. The status dot turns Green!

📑 App Tabs & Controls
🎾 Court Tab Live Match
• Live Score Mirror: Real-time digital display showing Points, Games, and Sets.
• Serve Indicator: Active server is highlighted with a 🎾 ball icon. Tap the [🎾 Serve: Home/Guests] badge to toggle who serves.
• Giant +1 Buttons: Large touch targets for one-handed point scoring.
• Quick Tools: Undo last point, Swap court sides, Toggle Clock/Score mode, and Reset match.
📺 Big Screen Spectator View Fullscreen
Tap the TV icon (📺) in the top header to enter dedicated fullscreen spectator scoreboard mode. High-contrast view designed for sideline tablets, club TVs, or audience phones.
📖 Rules Tab Game Config
Configure official padel match regulations:
• Golden Point: Toggle No-Ad (golden point at 40-40) or traditional Advantage/Deuce.
• Sets to Win: Best of 1, 3, or 5 sets.
• Tiebreak Length: Standard 7-point or 10-point super tiebreak.
• Save Rules: Pushes new rule set straight to scoreboard over BLE.
⏱️ Counter Tab Gym & Timers
Fitness and training timer modes:
• Single Timer: Count-Up stopwatch or Countdown timer with target minutes/seconds.
• Dual Timers: Two independent left and right clocks for drills or match interval pacing.
🕒 Clock Tab RTC Sync
• 1-Tap Sync: Syncs phone's precise real-time clock to the scoreboard's onboard DS3231 RTC chip.
• LED Color Customizer: Choose digit color from presets or the custom color wheel.
💡 Display Tab LED Hardware
• Brightness: Slider controls hardware LED brightness (0–100%).
• Inactivity Sleep: Auto-dim display after 1, 3, 5, 10, or 15 minutes of idle time.
⚙️ Settings Tab App Config
• Guides: Quick access to Mobile and Printable guides for App and Remote.
• QR Codes: Scan to share app URL or view official Padel rules.
• PWA Updates: Auto-update checker and cache reset.
• Theme & Simple/Advanced: Toggle Dark/Light theme, or switch between Simple (Court, Rules, Settings) and Advanced modes.
🌐 Browser Compatibility
Android, PC & Mac: Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Web Bluetooth connects natively without any extra app.
iPhone & iPad (iOS): Apple Safari blocks Web Bluetooth. Install the free Bluefy or WebBLE browser app from the App Store.
Offline PWA Ready: Install app to your home screen ("Add to Home Screen"). The app works completely offline at the court even without internet!
