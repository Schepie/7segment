The 4-Way Padel Ecosystem

The ESP32 Scoreboard acts as the authoritative Master Rules Engine. All wireless remotes, smartwatches, and spectator displays mirror the exact match state in real time.

📟

ESP32 Master Scoreboard

Hardware SPI DMA WS2812B bitstream driver ensures 100% flicker-free LED animations even during high-frequency BLE packet transfers. NVS flash memory restores match state across power cuts.

  • Official FIP rules: Golden Point, Tiebreak
  • Automated celebration & past set score cycles
  • Automatic idle fallback to real-time clock
🔘

Racket Clip-On Clicker

Ergonomic tactile remote mounted directly onto the racket handle or wrist strap. Score without looking at the board, breaking rhythm, or arguing between rallies.

  • Instant single-click scoring for both teams
  • Double-click mistake correction (30-step undo)
  • Over 6 months battery life on CR2032 coin cell

Wear OS Smartwatch App

Full haptic wrist feedback on points won, games won, and side swaps. Synchronizes master rules instantly over BLE GATT upon stepping onto the court.
