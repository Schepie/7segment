### What Actually Happened:

When your scoreboard booted up and showed the green **`CONN`**, **it did not connect to the ring — it connected to your old Xiaomi remote (`XiaoYi_RC`)!**

Here is the exact line from the board's serial log:
```text
[SCAN] 04:e6:76:b2:8a:b6 | RSSI: -68 | 'XiaoYi_RC' | Services: [0x1812 ... ]
[SCAN] ★ RECOGNIZED PAIRED REMOTE (04:e6:76:b2:8a:b6)! Stopping scan to connect...
[BLE] Connecting to 04:e6:76:b2:8a:b6...
[BLE] Successfully connected to remote!
```

Because the old Xiaomi remote is powered on nearby, the scoreboard recognized its saved MAC address (`04:e6:76:b2:8a:b6`), immediately re-connected to it, and **stopped scanning**.

When you saw **`CONN`** on the scoreboard and pressed the buttons on the ring, the scoreboard was actively listening to the old Xiaomi remote, not the ring.
