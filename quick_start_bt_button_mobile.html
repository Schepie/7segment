🔘 Bluetooth Remote
🎾 Remote Button Guide
Pocket Bluetooth remote controller for the ESP32 Padel Scoreboard. No phone required during gameplay!
⚡ 3-Step Instant Pairing
1

Power Scoreboard

Plug in display via USB-C (5V/3A). BLE automatically advertises ESP32_SCOREBOARD.

2

Wake Remote

Click either button once. The remote's blue LED flashes to wake from ultra-low power sleep.

3

Auto Connect

Scoreboard auto-links within ~2 seconds. Center LED flashes to confirm link is active.

🔘 Button Layout
Standard Bluetooth Shutter Remote • Wear on wrist or pocket clip
🎾 Padel Scoring Actions
👆 Single Click ● Big Button
+1 Point for Team 1 (Left / Blue)
Advances score: 0 → 15 → 30 → 40 → Game / Tiebreak.
👆 Single Click ■ Small Button
+1 Point for Team 2 (Right / Red)
Advances score: 0 → 15 → 30 → 40 → Game / Tiebreak.
✌️ Double Click (at 0–0) Either Button
Toggle 1st Server (Toss)
Before the match starts, double click to choose which team serves first. The golden pulsing LED on the central games ladder hops between Team 1 and Team 2!
✌️ Double Click (during match) Either Button
↩️ UNDO Last Point
Reverts the score back one point if an accidental click occurred. Center display flashes briefly.
⏱️ Long Press (at 0–0) Hold ≥ 1.5s
↔️ Swap Court Sides
Swaps Team 1 and Team 2 sides and display colors before the match starts.
⏱️ Long Press (during match) Hold ≥ 1.5s
🕒 Clock Mode Toggle
Shows current real-time clock (HH:MM). Click any button to resume the match score intact.
⏱️ Gym / Timer Mode
Single Timer Mode Countdown / Up
• Big Button: Toggle Start / Pause
• Small Button: Step value (+1 / -1)
• Double Click: Reset timer to target or zero
Dual Timer Mode Two Clocks
• Big Button: Start/Pause Left | Double Click: Reset Left
• Small Button: Start/Pause Right | Double Click: Reset Right
💡 Serve Indicator & Pro Tips
Gold Serve Pulse: On the scoreboard's middle ladder, the next unearned game LED gently pulses in Gold on the serving team's column.
Tiebreak Serves: In tiebreaks, 1 or 2 solid gold dots indicate whether the server is on their 1st or 2nd serve of the turn.
Battery Replacement: Uses a standard CR1632 3V coin cell (lasts 6–12 months). Twist open the back cap with a coin to swap.
Deep Sleep: The remote automatically sleeps after inactivity. The first press wakes it up and auto-reconnects in 1–2 seconds.
Undo Timing: Double-click threshold is 380ms. Keep a deliberate rhythm when scoring points quickly.
